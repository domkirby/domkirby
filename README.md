## 👋 About Me

I'm **Dom**, Sr. Director of Professional Services at **[Pax8](https://www.pax8.com)**, where I lead a team of engineers, strategists, and operations professionals delivering technical outcomes for thousands of managed service providers (MSPs).

I'm a cybersecurity practitioner, modern workplace connoisseur, husband, and father. My thing is connecting innovation to practical outcomes: strategies that work in the real world, not just on paper. I also advise vendors on security and AI, and share what I've learned on stage at conferences.

> *Views are my own and do not represent my employer or affiliates.*

## 🔒 Cybersecurity

Security for the SMB and MSP world is where I live. That means making good security **achievable** for organizations without enterprise budgets or headcount.

- 🛡️ Securing the modern workplace: identity, endpoints, and cloud
- 🟣 Sharpening my **purple teaming** skills, building offense and defense knowledge together
- 🧭 Turning complex security problems into simple, actionable guidance
- 🎓 Certified **CISSP** and **SSCP**

## 🤖 AI

I'm focused on how AI changes the way services are delivered **at scale**: where it genuinely helps MSPs and their customers, where it introduces new risk, and how to adopt it securely.

- ⚙️ Practical, security-minded AI adoption for SMBs
- 📈 Using AI to scale service delivery without losing the human element
- 🧪 Experimenting with new tools, then sharing what actually works
